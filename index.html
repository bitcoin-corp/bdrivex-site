How it works

Your files, your keys.

Like sending with a drive or a transfer site, except the file is locked before it leaves your device and only your link can unlock it.

  1. 1

    Sign in with your wallet

    Your bWalletX is your account. No email, no password. The people you send to don’t need a wallet at all.

    Beta
  2. 2

    Sealed on your device

    Each file is encrypted in your browser with its own key before a single byte is uploaded. Big files upload in pieces and carry on if the connection drops.

    Beta
  3. 3

    Send the link

    The key rides in the link, after the #, which browsers never send to a server. Choose how long it works, and switch it off any time.

    Beta
  4. 4

    Keep what matters

    Your files in one place on every device, paid for from your wallet a little each month. Stop paying and we stop storing.

    Next

We can’t open your files. We only ever hold scrambled bytes. Anyone you give the whole link to can download the file, and anyone can report a link that shouldn’t exist, which switches it off straight away.

Coming to bDriveX

Built for what comes next.

Pay as you go

Your wallet signs one payment for each month you want, in advance, and each one can only go out when its month comes round. Stop any time.

Recordings, with consent

Keep a Space recording only when every speaker agrees. It’s encrypted to the people in it, and its fingerprint is sealed on chain.

Storage for the b*X apps

bPhotosX keeps your photo library here, and bVideoX your videos: private by default, and sellable when you choose.
