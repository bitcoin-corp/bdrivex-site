How it works

Pay for storage as long as you like.

We keep your files and recordings. Your wallet signs one payment for each month you want, in advance, and each one is time-locked so it can only go out when its month comes round. Stop paying, and we stop storing.

Coming

Signed ahead, paid monthly

We can only collect a month when it arrives. Spend those coins elsewhere and the rest are cancelled.

Coming

Stop any time

If a month's payment can't be collected, you get a warning and time to top up. After that we delete the file. You're never locked in.

Coming

Recordings, with consent

Keep a Space recording only when every speaker agrees. It's encrypted to the people in it, its fingerprint is sealed on chain, and whoever wants it kept pays for it.

Your voice, your choice. A recording is kept only if everyone who spoke says yes. When a Space is transcribed, the audio used for the transcript is deleted afterwards.
