What bDriveX does

bDriveX lets you bring in files you pick (from Google Drive, from your device or from a Google Takeout ZIP), fingerprints them in your browser, and writes a record of each one to the Bitcoin SV blockchain in your own name, encrypted with your bWalletX keys. Your files stay where they are. We never download or host them.

Your wallet and keys

bWalletX keeps your keys on your device. We can’t see or recover your 12 words. If you lose them, nobody can open your private records. If they leak, whoever has them can.

Payments and the blockchain

Blockchain writes are permanent and can’t be reversed or refunded once sent. A fingerprint shows a file is the same one, byte for byte; it is not legal proof of who owns or wrote it.
